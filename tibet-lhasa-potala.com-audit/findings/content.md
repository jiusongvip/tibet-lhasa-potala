## Content Quality Findings

**Score: 78/100 (Weight: 23%)**

### What Works Well

- Substantive, long-form content on every page — typical articles run 1,500–3,000+ words with practical detail
- Strong E-E-A-T signals throughout: UNESCO World Heritage references, specific ticket prices (CNY), precise geographic coordinates, dated factual claims ("Updated August 2026")
- Natural, readable prose that avoids keyword stuffing while still covering relevant search terms
- Testimonials include specific visitor names, visit dates, and concrete details — builds trust
- 10-well-crafted FAQ questions on homepage covering the most common user queries
- Each sub-page covers its topic deeply with no detectable duplicate or thin content
- Excellent internal linking between related pages (e.g., visit ↔ permits ↔ travel-tips)
- Homepage includes urgency signals ("Daily cap of just 2,300 visitors") that encourage action

### Issues Found

| Severity | Finding |
|----------|---------|
| Critical | **No author/contributor bylines** — There is no author name, bio, or credentials on any page. For a YMYL-adjacent topic (international travel to high-altitude restricted area), Google's E-E-A-T guidelines strongly favor demonstrated expertise. The footer only states the site is "not affiliated with official tourism authorities" — but never says who *is* behind it. |
| High | **No About page or site ownership disclosure** — Visitors (and search engines) have no way to verify who operates the guide. An About page with author credentials, travel experience, and editorial standards would significantly boost E-E-A-T. |
| Medium | **Testimonials lack external verification** — While the testimonials include names and dates, there are no links to original reviews (TripAdvisor, Google Maps, etc.) and no Review schema markup to enable star ratings in SERPs. |
| Medium | **No "last reviewed" dates on content** — The homepage says "Updated August 2026" in the badge, but individual content sections and sub-pages do not display last-reviewed dates. Medical/altitude advice (Diamox recommendation, acclimatization timelines) should show review dates for trust. |
| Low | **No citations or sources** — Claims like "3,721 kilograms of gold" on the 5th Dalai Lama's stupa, "200,000 Buddhist statues," and historical dates lack linked references. Adding source links would improve E-E-A-T. |

### Recommendations

1. **Create an About page** with: author/editor credentials, travel experience in Tibet, editorial standards, and contact info. Add Organization schema.
2. **Add author bylines** to each article. Link to author bio pages or at minimum show a name + title.
3. **Add last-reviewed dates** to pages with health/safety content (altitude advice, Diamox recommendations).
4. **Link testimonials** to original sources or add Review schema markup.
5. **Add source citations** for key factual claims, especially historical and numerical ones.
