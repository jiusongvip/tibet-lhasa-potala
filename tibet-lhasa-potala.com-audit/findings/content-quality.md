## Key Findings

### Critical Issues

1. **Anonymous authorship** — No bylines, no About page, no credentials. The December 2025 core update tightened author attribution across ALL categories. A travel guide giving altitude/medical advice without named authors is a significant risk.

2. **Zero external citations** — Not one link to UNESCO, Wikipedia, official tourism board, CDC, or any authoritative source. A site claiming to be the "most complete English guide" should demonstrate its research.

3. **No first-person experience** — Content reads as thoroughly researched but never as personally experienced. "I visited in..." "When I climbed..." "In my experience..." — these phrases never appear.

### High-Impact Issues

4. **Medical advice without credentials** — Diamox recommendations, altitude sickness guidance, acclimatization timelines given without any medical disclaimer or author qualification.

5. **Repetitive structure** — 10 of 11 pages use identical "Frequently Asked Questions" H2 heading. All pages follow the identical PageHero → article → FAQ → PageNav template pattern.

6. **Image desert on sub-pages** — 10 pages have zero images. A travel guide without location photos is an experience-signal gap.

### Content Strengths

- Substantial word counts above minimums on every page
- Excellent internal linking (34-54 links per page)
- Specific, verifiable factual details throughout
- Clean heading hierarchy and scannable formatting
- FAQ content well-suited for AI citation
- Consistent freshness signals (2026 dates)

---

## Recommendations (Prioritized)

### Critical

1. **Create About page** with author identity, travel credentials, and editorial standards
2. **Add author bylines** to every page + Person schema
3. **Add medical disclaimer** to Travel Tips and altitude-related content

### High Priority

4. **Add 15-20 external citations** linking to authoritative sources (UNESCO, Wikipedia, CDC, official tourism board)
5. **Add first-person narrative** to 2-3 key sections — the strongest AI-content differentiator
6. **Vary FAQ headings** — replace generic "Frequently Asked Questions" on at least 5 pages
7. **Add images to sub-pages** — at least 1 relevant image per content page

### Medium Priority

8. **Add "Key Facts" boxes** to top of long articles (4-6 bullet points each)
9. **Add privacy policy page**
10. **Expand lighter pages** (Best Time, Attractions) by 300-500 words
11. **Add comparison tables** (hotels, transport, seasons)
